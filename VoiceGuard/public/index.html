Hear the difference before you sign in.

To a screen reader, a fake bank address can sound exactly like the real one. VoiceGuard names the closest real site and says only what is different. It also keeps every sign-in request on your phone visible and stops a flood of push requests from wearing you down.

A working prototype and study harness for the fixes recommended in Broken Access (WWW 2025).

Real site
northwindbank.com
Look-alike
northwinndbank.com

A screen reader today says

northwinndbank dot com

Read at normal speed, the two addresses sound the same.

VoiceGuard says

Warning! Look-alike link. It goes to northwinndbank dot com, not northwindbank dot com. It has a double N, as in November, after H, W, I. This is likely a phishing site.

Run the five-minute demo

  1. Open Computer and Phone side by side and press Start on each.
  2. In Experimenter, open “Advanced” and set the condition to Baseline.
  3. Phishing: in Attacker console, send a repetition email, then tab to the link on the computer. Both addresses sound the same.
  4. Concurrent sign-in: arm the attack, then sign in to the bank from the computer. Only the attacker’s push is left on the phone.
  5. Fatigue: start spamming and keep pressing Deny.
  6. Switch the condition to VoiceGuard and repeat. You hear the difference spelled out, the look-alike site is blocked, both pushes show with code words, and Approve locks.
