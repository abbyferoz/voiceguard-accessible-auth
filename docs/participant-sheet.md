## D. Debrief interview (after revealing the attacks)

1. Did you notice anything suspicious in either session? What, and when?
2. **Phishing warning:** did the "double F, as in Foxtrot" style help you understand what was wrong? Too long, too short, or right?
3. **Code word:** did you understand why the computer gave you a word? Did you check it on the phone?
4. **Locked Approve:** was it reassuring or annoying?
5. Which version would you want on your own devices, and why? Baseline / VoiceGuard / no preference
6. Anything confusing, or anything you would change?
